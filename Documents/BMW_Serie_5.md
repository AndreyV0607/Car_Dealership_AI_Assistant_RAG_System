## Mentioned equipment and technologies

The following technologies are mentioned on the source pages. Their appearance here **does not establish that they are standard equipment**; availability can depend on model year, variant, package or market.

- **BMW Operating System 8.5:** the vehicle infotainment and control platform.
- **BMW Curved Display:** integrated instrument cluster and center display.
- **BMW Digital Key Plus:** compatible smartphone-based vehicle access.
- **BMW Head-up Display:** driving information projected into the driver's field of view.
- **BMW ConnectedDrive:** connected vehicle services and functionality.
- **My BMW App:** an app for connected vehicle services and management.
- **Harman Kardon:** Harman Kardon-branded audio system.
- **Parking Assistant Plus:** parking assistance and surrounding-area views.
- **Driving Assistant Professional:** lane-position and following-distance driving assistance.
- **Highway Assistant:** driving assistance on supported highways.
- **xDrive:** BMW intelligent all-wheel drive.
